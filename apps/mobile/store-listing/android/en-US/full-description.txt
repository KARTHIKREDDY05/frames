Frames is a social scrapbook for everyday memories.

Capture photos and short videos throughout the day, share them for today, and keep them forever in a private scrapbook archive. Frames automatically turns posts into daily memory cards, monthly collages, and year-end stories so you can remember more without organizing albums by hand.

Key features:
- Capture photo and video Frames
- Choose Friends or Public privacy per post
- Browse a 24-hour social feed
- Keep every post in your private archive
- Open daily scrapbook cards
- Build monthly and yearly memory collections
- Export and share memories intentionally

Frames is designed around one principle: capture now, organize never, remember forever.
