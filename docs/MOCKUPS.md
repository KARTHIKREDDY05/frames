# UI Mockup Specs

Target size: `390 x 844`.

## Home Feed

Warm paper background, top title `Today`, vertically stacked scrapbook cards, avatar header, framed image, caption, privacy, reactions.

## Camera

Full-screen camera preview, compact top controls for flash and camera switch, large centered shutter button, gallery shortcut.

## Frame Editor

Captured photo lands inside a Polaroid, frame style chips below, caption input, privacy segmented control, `Post Frame`.

## Daily Frame

Date title, subtitle, cream scrapbook page with layered photos, tape, captions, location labels, and edit affordances.

## Archive

Private timeline grouped by month. Rows show date, weekday/subtitle, and frame count.

## Monthly Collage

Large image-led card with month title and compact stats: frames, places, friends, and short summary line.

## Yearbook

Book-like cover with year, title, cover image, and page-flip-ready reader surface.

## Profile

Avatar, display name, username, stats, edit/share/settings buttons, and highlight sections.
